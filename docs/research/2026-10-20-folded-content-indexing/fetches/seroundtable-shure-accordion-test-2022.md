# Another SEO Claims Higher Rankings Without Content Hidden In Tabs

> Fetched 2026-10-20 · Search Engine Roundtable, 2022-03-09. Read as the counter-claim to the 2018
> statement.

## What it says

Dan Shure, an SEO, made FAQ content that had been inside closed accordions visible by default, below
the fold on mobile and desktop. He reported a large ranking gain on competitive terms, with the
folded text already fully present in the HTML.

John Mueller (Google) replied: "I'm not convinced by these kinds of anecdotes, but YMMV."

## Caveat

One site, one change, no control. An anecdote, which is how Google answered it.
