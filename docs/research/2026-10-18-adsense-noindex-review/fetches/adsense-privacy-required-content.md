# Required content (AdSense Help)

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · The AdSense page listing what a publisher's privacy policy must say. Read so the
> paragraph added to `/privacy` in slice 1 quotes Google rather than memory. No last-updated date on
> the page.

## What it says

The privacy policy must include:

- "Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits" to
  the website or other websites.
- "Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users" based
  on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
- "Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting" Ads Settings
  (`https://www.google.com/settings/ads`). Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's
  cookies at `www.aboutads.info` (`http://www.aboutads.info/choices/`).

If the publisher has **not** opted out of third-party ad serving, the policy should also:

- notify visitors of the third-party vendors and ad networks serving ads on the site;
- link to those vendors' and networks' websites (the page names no specific URLs);
- tell users they can opt out of those vendors' personalized-advertising cookies there, or at
  `www.aboutads.info/choices/`.

Further reading it links: the Network Advertising Initiative (`http://www.networkadvertising.org/`)
and `cookiechoices.org`.

## What it does not say

- Whether funsona.com has third-party ad serving on or off. That is an account setting. The
  `/privacy` text covers both cases with the `aboutads.info` sentence, but does not list vendors by
  name.
