# AdSense for content ads on game play pages

> Fetched 2026-10-20 · AdSense Help. Read for where ads may sit relative to an interactive quiz,
> which bears on whether the player opens over the indexed landing page.

## What it says

- "We strongly recommend that Adsense for content ad units be placed at least 150 pixels away from
  the game". The page also suggests removing these ads from game play pages entirely.
- "Game play buttons should not lead to interstitial pages dominated by AdSense for content units"
- "Game play buttons should be clickable and placed in an obvious location."
- "Ad units should not surround game play links too closely."
