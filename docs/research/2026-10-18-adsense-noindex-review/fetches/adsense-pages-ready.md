# Make sure your site's pages are ready for AdSense

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · AdSense Help pre-signup checklist. Read hoping it describes how the site review
> samples pages.

## What it says

> "it needs to have unique content that's relevant to your visitors and provides a great user
> experience"

> "Valuable and original content is paramount to building a strong and loyal user base."

> "Google ads may not be placed on sites with scraped or copyrighted content."

> "This is a policy violation and could lead to you having your ads disabled or your account closed."

## What it does not say

- How Google reviews a site, or whether it judges the whole site or individual pages.
- Anything about auto-generated or templated content, `noindex`, unfinished sections or removing
  pages.
