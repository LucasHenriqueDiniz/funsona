# Mobile-first Indexing Best Practices

> Fetched 2026-10-20 · Google Search Central, page last updated 2025-12-10. Read to find out whether
> text inside a closed accordion is indexed.

## What it says

Under "Make sure that content is the same on desktop and mobile":

- "You can have a different design on mobile to maximize user experience (for example, moving content
  into accordions or tabs)"
- "Instead of removing content, consider moving content into accordions or tabs to save space."

Under "Make sure that Google can access and render your content":

- "Don't lazy-load primary content upon user interaction."
- "Google won't load content that requires user interactions (for example, swiping, clicking, or
  typing) to load."

## What it does not say

It does not name the HTML `<details>` element, and it says nothing about how much ranking weight
folded text gets.
