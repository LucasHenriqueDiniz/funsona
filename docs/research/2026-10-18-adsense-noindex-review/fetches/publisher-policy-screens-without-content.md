# Google-served ads on screens without publisher-content

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Google Publisher Policies. This is the policy behind "low value content". Read
> for its scope: is it about pages that carry ads, or about the whole site?

## What it says

> "We do not allow Google-served ads on screens:" — without publisher-content or with low-value
> content; under construction; used for alerts, navigation or other behavioral purposes.

> "The content you provide should be of value to the user and be the focal point for users visiting
> your site or app."

Google may limit or disable ad serving on pages with low-value content or still being constructed,
until changes are made.

No ads on "dead end" or no-content screens (Thank You, Exit, Error pages).

> "Don't place ads on automatically generated content without manual review or curation."

## What it does not say

- Whether it is enforced per screen or across the site. The wording is per screen ("ads on screens",
  "pages with low value content").
- Nothing about templated content by name. Examples 1 and 2 are images and were not captured.
- Nothing about `noindex`.
