# Site not ready to show ads (AdSense Help)

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · The help page a "Requires attention" status points to. Read for its definition
> of low value content and the re-review procedure.

## What it says

Section heading: "Your site doesn't have enough unique content or provide a good user experience."

> "Before you can start monetizing a site with AdSense, it's important that your site provides enough
> valuable content to users"

The site should have "a good user experience and navigational elements."

> "When you've addressed the above points, submit your site for review."

The review "usually takes a few days, but in some cases can take 2-4 weeks."

Procedure: Sites → select the site → confirm the code is on the site → Next → Request review.

## What it does not say

- The term "low value content" does not appear. No definition, no examples (scraped, auto-generated,
  templated).
- Nothing about how pages are sampled, or about `noindex`.
