# Google Search Central — Spam policies for Google web search

> Fetched 2026-11-17 (HTTP 200, read in full). Read for the two policies the seed catalogue's
> history matches. No last-updated date was shown. An earlier copy of the scaled-content section is
> `../../2026-10-18-adsense-noindex-review/fetches/search-spam-policies-scaled-content.md`.

## What it says

- **Scaled content abuse:** "Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary
  purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users." One listed example is "Scraping
  feeds, search results, or other content to generate many pages (including through automated
  transformations like synonymizing, translating, or other obfuscation techniques)".
- **Scraping:** "Scraping refers to the practice of taking content from other sites". One listed
  example is copying content from other sites and modifying it "only slightly (for example, by
  substituting synonyms or using automated techniques)".

## What it does not say

- Any threshold: how many pages, or what share of a site, makes a pattern "scaled".
- Translation is named under scaled content abuse, not under scraping.
